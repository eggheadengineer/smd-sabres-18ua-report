All games 3‑3‑2 Division 1‑2‑2, 4th of 6 Goals 25 for, 13 against
The need is the puck

The Sabres spend too much of each game defending. Against 18UA/AA teams they get only 40% of the shots, they're outshot in every period, and the 2nd period is the worst. The goalies have covered for it, but late in games the chances against get better and goals follow: 6 of the 9 division goals against have come in the 3rd. A short bench of 11 or 12 skaters a night is a likely cause, and scoring leans on four forwards.

What they need

  1. More skaters.Getting to 15 or more a night is the most direct way to spend less time defending and to have fresh legs in the 3rd.
  2. A forward who drives play.Someone who carries the puck into the offensive zone and keeps it there, adding scoring beyond Alpert, Lea, Willett and Nguyen.
  3. A working power play.Opponents have given them plenty of chances: 18 minors in 7 games, 1 power‑play goal.
  4. Goaltending is not a need.The team saves about 93% of shots, which keeps outshot games close.

What the Sabres can do

  1. Start fast.Frederick has trailed after the 1st in six of nine games. An early goal puts them in a hole they haven't climbed out of often.
  2. Get pucks and bodies to the net.Dockens is their strength. Clean shots from outside won't beat him; screens and rebounds will.
  3. Make the power play count.Frederick takes almost five penalties a game. That's the Sabres' best chance yet to get the power play going.
  4. Manage the bench.Frederick will likely have 3 or 4 more skaters and plays its best hockey in the 3rd. Short shifts matter.
