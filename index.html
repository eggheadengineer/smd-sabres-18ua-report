Posted games 5‑3‑3 Regular season 3‑2‑3 Goals 34 for, 14 against
The need is the puck

The Sabres still spend too much of each game defending. Against 18UA/AA teams they get 40% of the shots and are outshot in every period; even in Saturday's 3–1 win over first‑place Baltimore they were outshot 27–18. The goalies are why the results have turned: one goal allowed on 61 shots in the last two league games, and no 3rd‑period goals on 24 shots. A short bench of 12 skaters a night is a likely cause of the shot gap, and scoring still leans on four forwards. Sunday's 6–0 win over Navy came against a lower‑tier team and doesn't change that.

What they need

  1. More skaters.Getting to 15 or more a night is the most direct way to spend less time defending and to have fresh legs in the 3rd.
  2. A forward who drives play.Someone who carries the puck into the offensive zone and keeps it there, adding scoring beyond Alpert, Lea, Willett and Nguyen.
  3. A working power play.3 for 11 in league games. Baltimore took 10 penalties on Nov 7 and the Sabres scored once.
  4. Goaltending is not a need.The team saves about 94% of shots, which keeps outshot games close.
