The need is depth

The goalies are the team's strength. The problem is a short bench. The Sabres have dressed 10 to 13 skaters a game, and in division games they've given up 6 of their 9 goals against in the 3rd period. They've had the lead or a tie going into the 3rd in four division games and won none of them. Scoring leans on four forwards.

2 goals a game against the six 18UA/AA opponents. Four players have 20 of the 25 goals.

Latest game

Oct 17, at Jr. Black Bears: L 1–3. Noah Alpert scored in the 1st (from Willett and Lea). Jr. Black Bears tied it in the 2nd and scored twice in the 3rd, the last with 2:34 left. What they need

  1. More skaters.Getting to 15 or more a night is the single biggest fix. Two‑thirds of the division goals against have come in the 3rd period.
  2. Another scoring forward.Four forwards carry the scoring. One more who can score would let a second line take real minutes.
  3. A working power play.Opponents have given them plenty of chances: 18 minors in 7 games, 1 power‑play goal.
  4. Goaltending is not a need.The team saves about 93% of shots, which keeps outshot games close.
