The need is the puck

Two losses this weekend, 2–3 to Frederick and 0–2 at first‑place Tri‑City, and the same problem underneath. Against 18UA/AA teams the Sabres get 42% of the shots. Saturday was an exception: they outshot Frederick 24–21, the first time all season they've outshot a UA/AA team, and still lost because Frederick's Noah Dockens has now stopped 46 of 48 Sabres shots in two games. Sunday they dressed only 9 skaters and were outshot 31–25. The goalies keep games close (.937 overall), but the offense has 2 goals in the last two games, and 14 of the 17 goals against UA/AA teams have come from the same four forwards.

What they need

  1. More skaters.They dressed 9 at Tri‑City. Getting to 15 or more a night is the most direct way to spend less time defending and to have fresh legs in the 3rd.
  2. A forward who drives play.Someone who carries the puck into the offensive zone and keeps it there, adding scoring beyond Alpert, Lea, Willett and Nguyen.
  3. A working power play.4 power‑play goals in 13 posted games while opponents took 48 penalties.
  4. Goaltending is not a need.The team saves about 94% of shots, which keeps outshot games close.
